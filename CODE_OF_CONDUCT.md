# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique ideas not people —
disagreeing with a rule is welcome (that is what the issue templates are
for); disrespecting a person is not.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via
GitHub. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
